      Vision pipeline (OpenCV 5)

      1. Find the rim. Median of the first frames, CLAHE, Canny, findContours + fitEllipse, scored by edge support. Refined to the inner edge of the bright lip by a per-angle sub-pixel fit.
      2. Unwrap. remap into an elliptical polar image, so a rising surface is a front moving outward.
      3. Stabilise. Rim-anchored phaseCorrelate with a Hanning window cancels hood vibration; hands crossing the rim are rejected.
      4. Normalise light and steam. Trimmed per-angle regression on the rim lip gives gain/offset; low gain = steam.
      5. Find the foam front. The inner wall is static background: a vectorised change-point search per angle, median over angles.
      6. Depth. Pinhole geometry: d = H (r_rim / r_surface − 1).
      7. Agitation. Farneback optical flow flags boiling onset.

      Prediction and action

      1. Physics. Foam grows exponentially then saturates. cv2.KalmanFilter tracks log-foam, growth rate and its change.
      2. Uncertainty. Monte-Carlo samples of the posterior give P(overflow within the hob's heat lag).
      3. Act. Alert, then lower heat via AWS IoT Core before the lag runs out (with cancel window / approval modes).
      4. Verify. Watch that foam growth reverses; if not, burner off + alarm.
      5. See better. Steam hides the rim → switch the hood fan on.
      6. Explain. Amazon Bedrock (Nova) writes the notification and incident report from the numbers only.
